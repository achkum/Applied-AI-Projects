## Goal

Replace the original "Norrsken" (Northern Lights / orbital) concept with a production-grade design language the founder calls "Precision" — modelled after Klarna, Kivra, and Revolut. Rewrite the interactive prototype to reflect the new direction and make it fully functional.

## Context

Founder feedback 2026-09-28: *"the UI feels very kiddish and vibe coded. not at all like a production app. look at klarna, kivra and other leading apps and try to imitate them instead."*

Specific complaints that drove the pivot:
- Orbital canvas animation, aurora gradients, glassmorphism
- Purple used everywhere (background fills, bars, avatars)
- Decorative header "SubTrack · Interactive Prototype"
- Theme/language controls floating outside the app instead of inside Profile
- Member filter pills not wired to data
- Category filter chips not filtering subscription rows
- Only 2 insight cards (now 6 action + 4 market)
- Home insight section text-only with no data visualisations
- Tab bar navigation felt static (opacity fade, no slide)

## What changed

### Design language (DESIGN_DIRECTION.md)
- Retired: orbital solar-system metaphor, Fraunces/Manrope fonts, aurora gradients, glassmorphism, glowing indicators
- New: flat 1px-border cards, Inter Variable font, single desaturated accent (#7b6fe8), tabular-nums amounts, production-style list rows
- Three surface levels (canvas/surface/card) with full dark + light token sets

### Prototype (docs/subtrack-prototype.html)
- Complete rewrite from ~1,600 to ~870 lines
- All 5 screens (Hem, Prenumerationer, Hushåll, Insikter, Profil) fully designed
- **Navigation architecture**: 5 duplicate nav copies → single global `#main-nav` with `data-tab` attributes
- **Tab transitions**: opacity fade → slide `translateX` with `cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1)`
- **Insights tab**: badge showing 4 unresolved action items
- **Home interactivity**: upcoming rows → drawer, savings card → Insights, stat chips → Insights/Household, "See all →" links
- **Member pills**: wired to MEMBERS data object; switching member updates hero total and subscription count
- **Category filter chips**: wired to `data-cat` attributes on subscription rows
- **Period toggle**: monthly/annual with live hero update
- **Theme/language**: moved into Profile settings rows (toggleTheme, toggleLang)
- **Subscription drawer**: opens for all 7 subs, fully populated from SUBS data object
- **Market intelligence section**: 4 cards with external competitor/price signal insights
- **Savings visualisation**: comparison bar chart on home screen
- **Stat chips**: 4 chips with semantic colours, all tappable
- **Full i18n**: sv + en for all strings including new keys
