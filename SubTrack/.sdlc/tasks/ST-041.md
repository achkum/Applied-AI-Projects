## Goal
Deliver email/sms otp service with abuse controls in scope of SubTrack PRODUCT_SPEC, with privacy and security invariants enforced.
## Context
- PRODUCT_SPEC §F1.1; USER_FLOWS.md UF-01 steps 2–3; DATA_MODEL.md §Identity; ADR-0003
## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given a valid email or E.164 phone, when OTP is requested, then a six-digit challenge expires in 10 minutes and is delivered via the configured SMTP or DevInbox adapter.
- AC2: Given resend requests, when fewer than 30 seconds elapsed or the sixth request occurs within one hour, then reject; otherwise allow up to five resends/hour.
- AC3: Given five incorrect codes, when another verify is attempted, then identifier is locked for 15 minutes; correct unexpired code succeeds once and is consumed.
- AC4: Given any log/event, when challenge data is recorded, then plaintext OTP and PII are absent.
## Open decision / gate
- A6 is Assumed and <48 h old; route D-12 through the Conductor before selecting/creating an external OTP provider. D-03/D-09 prohibit unapproved account creation and real delivery to non-founder recipients; Mailpit/DevInbox remain available.
