Backfill the device-dependent evidence that ST-024/025/026/027 could not produce: Maestro E2E (iOS simulator + Android emulator), real screen-reader sessions (VoiceOver/TalkBack), and device-based performance profiling for Orbit — none of which are achievable by an agent in the current execution environment (no simulators/emulators/physical devices/Maestro binary present).

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: A CI or local environment exists that can run iOS Simulator and Android Emulator sessions (this may require infrastructure work — a Mac runner or emulator-capable CI image — scope that first; do not fabricate results against a missing environment).
- AC2: Maestro E2E flows for all four components' interactions (RollingNumber value-change, ScopeSwitcher scope-change, ReceiptCard tap, Orbit tap/long-press/rotation) pass on both iOS and Android.
- AC3: At least one real VoiceOver (iOS) and one real TalkBack (Android) session per component, with recorded notes or a session recording, confirming label pronunciation, focus order, and reduced-motion behavior.
- AC4: Orbit performance profiling on a mid-range Android device (e.g. Pixel 4a) and iOS (iPhone 12+) confirms the 60fps target from ST-027 AC5, or documents the actual measured frame rate and any follow-up needed.
- AC5: Web cross-browser matrix (Chrome/Firefox/Safari) for Orbit (ST-025 AC5) and Playwright/axe-playwright E2E + pixel-level visual-regression snapshots for all D1 components (ST-024/025 deferred evidence), wired via ST-017's CI job.
