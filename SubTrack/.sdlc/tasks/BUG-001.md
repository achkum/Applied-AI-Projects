## Goal
Restore one compiling and tested domain visibility policy API whose behavior and types match the accepted DATA_MODEL visibility rule, while preserving the tests and privacy guarantees.

## Context (read before starting)
- DATA_MODEL.md “Visibility rule” and Constitution Article 3.2
- ST-048 contract and both current policy implementations
- `packages/domain/test/visibility.spec.ts` currently imports `canViewSubscription`, `canViewBankingData`, and `canReadAuditLog` that the policy barrel does not export; the legacy `visibility.ts` API/types also diverge from the newer `subscription-visibility.ts` context model.
- The discrepancy is policy-sensitive: do not choose one API or behavior by inference. Before implementation, obtain architect-platform and security-privacy review of DATA_MODEL and ST-048 against the current implementations/tests. If these sources conflict or require a product/architecture change, stop and return BLOCKED with the conflict; do not silently rewrite the policy or spec.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: After the required architect-platform and security-privacy review, the implementation exposes one coherent exported policy API and context types; obsolete duplicate behavior is removed or made a compatible wrapper only when reviewers confirm compatibility with DATA_MODEL and ST-048.
- AC2: Subscription visibility grants access only under the accepted DATA_MODEL rules: owner; active explicit share in a household where requester is active; or common active household where owner has open_book and the subscription is not always_private.
- AC3: Banking transactions/accounts and other always-private categories remain inaccessible to non-owners, irrespective of household membership, open_book, or subscription sharing. Preserve the currently tested active-household-admin-only `canReadAuditLog` behavior; do not broaden audit-log access in this task.
- AC4: Existing applicable unit/property and RLS-parity tests are preserved and corrected only where the review establishes a test/API mismatch. No tests are deleted, skipped, or marked `xfail` to obtain a green run.
- AC5: The domain package typechecks and its complete test suite passes. Policy branch coverage is 100% per Constitution Article 3.3.6 and QUALITY_GATES.md.
- AC6: Handoff records the architect-platform and security-privacy policy review evidence, tests/coverage commands, and any follow-up decision needed.

## Out of scope
- Changing DATA_MODEL.md, ST-048, database/RLS schema, or product privacy behavior without an explicit reviewed decision.
- API endpoint, UI, or persistence changes; changing unrelated policy behavior.

## Technical notes
