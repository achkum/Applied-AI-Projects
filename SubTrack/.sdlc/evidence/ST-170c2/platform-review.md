HMAC key and binding digests are domain-separated; binding frames operation, transport, authority kind/digest, and canonical request digest. Frozen snapshots and records support the intended immutability checks.

Reservation and settlement are synchronous `Map` transitions and therefore atomic within one process. Duplicate keys stay blocked while pending and after either terminal outcome until expiry. Settlement checks the original binding, pending state, expiry, and owner hash. Clock, TTL, expiry, and capacity checks fail closed; expired rows are pruned before capacity evaluation.
