# ST-215 security/privacy contract readiness — 2026-10-10

Decision: APPROVE the bounded, contract-only status PATCH reconciliation; no new audit event, replay receipt, idempotency persistence, or retention state is required for this slice.
Basis: USER_FLOWS UF-08 lists pause/cancel/archive without an audit AC; PRODUCT_SPEC F5.5 defines lifecycle, while F2 sharing and UF-05 privacy explicitly require audit. ARCHITECTURE lists audit for auth/sharing/privacy/membership/exports/deletions. ADR-0016 §§1,5,6 reserves atomic audit/receipt/replay for future review/edit/undo and blocks their persistence pending retention and DB proof; §3 does not advertise ordinary UF-08 archive/cancel as undoable.
Scope: exactly seven ACTIVE/PAUSED/CANCELLED transition pairs in architecture-next-plan §3; locked current status determines the action, and every other pair (including self, DETECTED, TRIAL, REJECTED and ARCHIVED) conflicts. Accept only a UUID path and exactly `{status}` with ACTIVE/PAUSED/CANCELLED/ARCHIVED; reject unknown, mixed, owner/action/scope/privacy/price/date fields and query selectors.
Authority: accepted V1 current-session Bearer guard/resolver/owner reader and transaction-local identical `app.user_id`/`app.current_user_id` under RLS. Caller cannot nominate principal. Missing and owner-invisible resources share the same 404 body; responses disclose only id/status.
Contract errors: RFC 9457 400 malformed path/body/target/query, 401 invalid current principal, 404 `SUBSCRIPTION_NOT_FOUND`, 409 `LIFECYCLE_CONFLICT`, masked 500. Bound request/error fixtures should prove exact allowlist, invalid combinations, security scheme and no private error details.
Retry: a committed PATCH whose response is lost may yield 409 on repeat; client re-reads authoritative state. No replay or exactly-once claim. ADR-0016's future receipt semantics remain separate.
Limit: this approves contract authoring only. It grants no route/runtime authority, no new identity model, and no ST-081/UF-06 completion. Runtime still requires accepted ST-081c, current-principal binding, ordinary least-privilege PostgreSQL RLS/concurrency/rollback proof and independent security review at the exact head.
