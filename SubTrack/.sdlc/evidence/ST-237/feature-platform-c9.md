## Blocking finding

**Major — startup does not qualify the runtime role that owns every private profile transaction.** `apps/web/private-https-server.mjs:348-375` checks attributes for only `subtrack_auth_owner`, `subtrack_auth_executor`, `subtrack_auth_producer`, and `subtrack_dev_auth_login`, and checks membership only for the login and three auth roles. It never checks `subtrack_runtime` attributes or memberships. The launcher itself executes `SET LOCAL ROLE subtrack_runtime` at `:516`; `DurableCurrentWebAuthority` does so for each current-web operation. A post-install `ALTER ROLE subtrack_runtime BYPASSRLS` or `SUPERUSER`, or a privileged membership granted to that role, leaves the enumerated function/profile policy/ACL/constraint catalog unchanged and can let the startup gate pass with owner RLS ineffective. The reviewed ordinary migration creates `subtrack_runtime NOLOGIN NOINHERIT NOSUPERUSER NOBYPASSRLS` (`0002_privacy_constraints/migration.sql:159`). Pin those actual attributes and the relevant role-membership graph before `server.listen`, and prove startup refusal for at least a runtime-role privilege mutation in the disposable installation. Restore the role in the proof before subsequent cases. This is part of the required private writer graph, not an optional hardening item.
