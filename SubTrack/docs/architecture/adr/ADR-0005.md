# ADR-0005: BankDataProvider abstraction; Synthetic provider first, Tink Sandbox second

- Status: Accepted (seed ADR, founder may veto per ASSUMPTIONS.md)
- Deciders: architect-platform, Conductor

## Context
Tink credentials don't exist yet. The product must be demoable with rich data regardless.

## Decision
Build SyntheticBankProvider in M2 as a first-class provider. The Tink adapter follows a documented capability discovery against the live sandbox docs/account; no invented fields.

## Consequences
+ No external blocker. − Risk of synthetic/Tink shape mismatch, mitigated by the shared normaliser and the Tink discovery report.
